# Librarian pass: the WP-RH lane against the live literature

Date: 2026-08-07. Scope: the transfer-law conjecture and its evidence chain
(RH4–RH7) plus the lane's earlier claims, searched against the live web
(searches recorded below). Purpose: the audit ladder's librarian stage,
applied without mercy, before any paper. Access caveat recorded honestly:
several full texts (arXiv PDFs, Project Euclid) returned 403 through this
environment's proxy, so some verdicts are provisional pending full-text reads;
those are flagged.

## Verdicts, claim by claim

1. **Prime peaks in the Fourier analysis of the zeros — KNOWN (existence).**
   Odlyzko's classic computations exhibit the Fourier transform of the zeta
   zeros with sharp features at every logarithm of a prime and prime power and
   nowhere else; the distributional statement is the Guinand/Weil picture and
   the quasicrystal reading is Dyson's. Any paper must introduce our
   detections as *quantifying a phenomenon known since Landau and made vivid
   by Odlyzko*, never as discovering the peaks.

2. **The continuous full-amplitude content (RH6, Part B) — CLASSICAL.**
   The Landau–Gonek theorem gives, under RH,
   `Σ_{0<γ<T} x^{iγ} = −(T/2π)Λ(x)/√x + E(x,T)` — full von Mangoldt
   amplitude. Our H1 checks (ratios 0.97–1.00 over 500 zeros) are a numerical
   verification of Landau–Gonek, and must be cited as such (Landau 1912;
   Gonek's uniform version; recent generalizations).

3. **The attenuated object — ADJACENT LITERATURE EXISTS; provisional.**
   Our attenuation measurement is, in classical terms, a *discrete twisted
   moment of S at the zeros* — the regression of `S̄(γ_n)` against
   `sin(γ_n ln m)`, i.e. the object `Σ S̄(γ) x^{iγ}`. This sits directly in
   the ambit of the Landau–Gonek generalization literature (a 2026 arXiv
   paper "Generalisations of the Landau–Gonek Theorem and Applications to
   Mean Values of Zeta" could not be read past its listing — 403), of Fujii's
   "On a theorem of Landau" I–II, and of the modern rigorous work on Berry's
   number-variance conjecture (e.g. arXiv 2211.14918). **No published
   quantitative attenuation law or Debye–Waller reading for this object was
   found in any search ("Debye-Waller" + zeta: no hits), but the full texts
   above MUST be read before a paper asserts the law is untabulated.** Until
   then the claim is "we are not aware of a published quantitative law," with
   the adjacent works cited.

4. **Cross-family universality of the constant (RH5b) — a measured face of a
   THEOREM.** Rudnick–Sarnak (Duke 1996) prove n-level correlations of
   cuspidal automorphic L-functions are universal (GUE) in restricted
   regimes. If the transfer law derives from pair correlation (the lane's
   derivation target), the family-independence of `a` is *expected* from
   Rudnick–Sarnak. The paper must present RH5b as a quantitative face of
   their universality, cited as the ADOPTED foundation — not as a surprise.

5. **Overtones at prime powers (RH7) — the known home is Bogomolny–Keating.**
   BK's lower-order, arithmetic corrections to pair correlation ("beyond the
   diagonal approximation"; derived alternatively from Hardy–Littlewood)
   are precisely where prime-power arithmetic enters zero statistics below
   the universal terms, and were developed to explain structure in Odlyzko's
   data. Our overtone existence table is plausibly a displacement-sequence
   shadow of BK lower-order terms; the paper must cite BK and frame the
   measurement accordingly. The specific out-of-sample overtone table
   (25, 27 predicted and confirmed; height decay) appears untabulated —
   provisional, same access caveat.

6. **The semiclassical potential and its residual (RH2) — owned as already
   framed** (Wu–Sprung; Schumayer–Hutchinson; Berry–Keating), no change.

7. **The preregistration registry applied to zeta numerics — no prior art
   surfaced.** The methodological contribution (frozen gates, refutations as
   permanent records, out-of-sample promotion of claims) appears genuinely
   novel in this literature and is the paper's safest headline contribution.

## Consequences for the paper

- Reframe the narrative: the paper *quantifies known phenomena under a new
  discipline* — amplitude law + controls + preregistration + cross-family
  and out-of-sample tests — and states one conjecture (the DW transfer law)
  whose rigorous home is identified (discrete twisted moments / pair
  correlation) but not yet occupied, so far as we could verify.
- Mandatory reading before submission (full texts, flagged by the 403s):
  the Landau–Gonek generalizations paper (arXiv 2601.18025); Fujii, "On a
  theorem of Landau" I–II (Proc. Japan Acad.); "On the number variance of
  zeta zeros and a conjecture of Berry" (arXiv 2211.14918); Odlyzko's 1987
  Math. Comp. paper and his "Primes, quantum chaos and computers" essay
  (to pin exactly which Fourier object his figures show); Bogomolny–Keating
  (Phys. Rev. Lett. 1996 / Nonlinearity).
- New citations to add to any paper draft: Landau (1912), Gonek's uniform
  Landau formula, Rudnick–Sarnak (1996), Bogomolny–Keating, Odlyzko (1987 +
  essay), Fujii, and the modern number-variance work.
